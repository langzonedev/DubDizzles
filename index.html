Controls & field manual +

Desktop: WASD move, mouse look (click battlefield to capture cursor), arrows turn, click or Space fire, right mouse or Q aim, Shift sprint, R reload, G grenade, B place cover, E resupply by a green crate, Esc or Tab pause.

Touch: left stick moves; push fully forward to sprint. Drag the right side to look. Tap AIM to toggle aiming. Use action buttons to fire, reload, resupply, build or throw a grenade. Landscape recommended.

Zone control and eliminations earn cash. Paid weapons cost cash each life. Green crates restore health, ammunition and grenades. Cover costs $100; grenades destroy orange cover. Cash and your best score save on this device. No real purchases.
